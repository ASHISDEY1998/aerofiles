# Privacy Policy for AeroFiles

**Last updated:** November 2026

AeroFiles ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using our mobile application.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
* **No Personal Data Collected:** AeroFiles does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable information, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics with any third parties or external servers.
* **Local Operation:** All file operations, indexing, media playback, and storage analysis are performed locally and on-device.

### 2. Device Permissions
AeroFiles requests the following permissions strictly to provide core file manager capabilities:
* **All Files Access (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** Required to allow users to browse, create, move, copy, rename, delete, and restore files across internal storage and connected storage media.
* **Network & Wi-Fi State (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`):** Required exclusively to connect to user-configured local network shares (SMB, WebDAV, SFTP, FTP) and to provide local Wi-Fi browser file management ("Access from PC"). No data is transmitted to our servers.

### 3. Network Storage and Credentials
* Any network connection credentials (server addresses, usernames, passwords) entered by the user are stored encrypted/locally in private application storage on your device. Credentials are never sent to external third parties.

### 4. Third-Party Services
AeroFiles does not embed third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or analytics services.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the application and repository.
